Currency Converter

About Currency Conversion

Currency conversion allows you to calculate the value of one currency in terms of another using real-time exchange rates. It is widely used for international trade, travel, and online transactions.

How It Works:

  • Exchange rates are determined by global financial markets
  • Rates change frequently based on demand and supply
  • Online converters fetch live data from financial APIs

Popular Currency Codes:

  • USD – US Dollar
  • INR – Indian Rupee
  • EUR – Euro
  • GBP – British Pound
  • JPY – Japanese Yen

Tips:

  • Always verify currency codes before converting
  • Rates may vary slightly between banks
  • Use real-time conversion for accuracy
