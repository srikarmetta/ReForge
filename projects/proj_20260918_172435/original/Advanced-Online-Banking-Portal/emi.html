Loan EMI Calculator

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount a borrower pays every month to repay a loan. It includes both the principal amount and the interest charged by the bank.

How EMI is Calculated:

EMI is calculated using a standard mathematical formula that depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and tenure of the loan.

EMI Formula:

EMI = [P × R × (1 + R)N] / [(1 + R)N − 1]

Where:

  • P = Loan Amount
  • R = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • N = Number of Monthly Installments (Years × 12)

Example:

  • Loan = ₹1,00,000
  • Interest Rate = 10% per year
  • Tenure = 2 years

The EMI is calculated using the formula above and remains constant every month.

Why EMI is Important:

  • Helps plan monthly budget
  • Ensures disciplined repayment
  • Used for loans like home, car, education
  • Easy comparison of loan options
