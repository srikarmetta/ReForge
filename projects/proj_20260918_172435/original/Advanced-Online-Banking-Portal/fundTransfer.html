About Fund Transfer in Net Banking

Net banking allows you to transfer money securely from your bank account to another account anytime, anywhere. It is fast, convenient, and eliminates the need to visit a bank branch.

Types of Fund Transfer:

  • NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer): Used for small to medium transfers. Works in batches.
  • RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement): Used for large transfers (₹2 lakh and above). Instant processing.
  • IMPS (Immediate Payment Service): Instant transfer available 24/7, even on holidays.

Important Points:

  • Ensure correct account number and IFSC code before transferring.
  • Transactions are secure and encrypted.
  • Some transfers may take time depending on bank processing.
  • Always keep your login credentials confidential.
