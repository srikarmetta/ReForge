Loan Eligibility Checker

What is a Loan?

A loan is an amount of money borrowed from a bank or financial institution that must be repaid over time with interest. Common types of loans include home loans, car loans, personal loans, and education loans.

What is Loan Eligibility?

Loan eligibility determines whether a person qualifies for a loan based on their financial condition. Banks evaluate factors like income, existing debts, and repayment capacity before approving a loan.

How We Check Eligibility:

In this system, loan eligibility is calculated based on your monthly income and existing EMI obligations.

  • We assume that a maximum of 40%–50% of your income can be used for loan repayment
  • If your current EMI is low compared to your income, you are eligible
  • If your EMI is too high, you may not be eligible for additional loans

Example:

  • Monthly Income = ₹50,000
  • Maximum EMI allowed (50%) = ₹25,000
  • If existing EMI = ₹10,000 → Eligible
  • If existing EMI = ₹30,000 → Not Eligible

Why This Matters:

  • Prevents financial burden
  • Ensures timely repayment
  • Helps banks reduce risk
  • Improves financial planning
