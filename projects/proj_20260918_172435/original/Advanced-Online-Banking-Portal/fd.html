FD Calculator

💡 Fixed Deposit (FD) is a banking investment where money is deposited for a fixed time at a fixed interest rate.

💰 Maturity Amount: It is the final amount you receive after the FD period ends. It includes your original deposit (principal) + the interest earned over time.

📌 In simple words: Maturity Amount = Principal + Interest Earned
📌 Formula Used:
A = P(1 + r/100)^t
Interest = A - P
