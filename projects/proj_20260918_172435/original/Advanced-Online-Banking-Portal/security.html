Bank Security & Data Protection

1. Data Encryption

We use 256-bit SSL encryption to protect your data during online transactions.

All sensitive information is encrypted before transmission.

2️. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Every login requires OTP verification sent to your registered mobile number or email.

This prevents unauthorized access even if password is compromised.

3️. Secure Payment Gateway

Our payment systems comply with PCI-DSS standards.

All online payments are monitored in real-time for suspicious activities.

4️. Fraud Detection System

  • AI-based transaction monitoring
  • Instant alerts for unusual transactions
  • Automatic account blocking on suspicious activity

5️. Account Protection Features

  • Login attempt limit
  • Session timeout after inactivity
  • CAPTCHA verification
  • Secure password requirements

6️. User Security Tips

  • Never share your OTP or password.
  • Always log out after completing transactions.
  • Use strong passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking transactions.

7️. Regulatory Compliance

Our banking platform follows RBI guidelines and international cybersecurity standards.

Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments are conducted.
