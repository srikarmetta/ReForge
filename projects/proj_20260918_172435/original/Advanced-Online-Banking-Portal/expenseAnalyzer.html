Expense Analyzer

    About Expense Analysis

    Expense analysis helps you track and manage your daily spending. It allows better financial planning and helps avoid unnecessary expenses.

    How It Works:

    • Each expense is recorded with amount and category
    • Total expense is calculated automatically
    • Data is stored locally for future use

    Benefits:

    • Helps control spending habits
    • Improves savings
    • Identifies unnecessary expenses
    • Useful for budgeting
