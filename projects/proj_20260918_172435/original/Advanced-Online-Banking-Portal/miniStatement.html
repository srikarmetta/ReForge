Mini Statement

    About Bank Statements

    A bank statement is a summary of all transactions made in your account over a specific period. A mini statement shows only the most recent transactions for quick reference.

    Key Features:

    • Shows last few transactions instantly
    • Helps track recent spending
    • Available anytime via net banking
    • Useful for quick balance checks

    Important Tips:

    • Always review transactions regularly
    • Report unknown transactions immediately
    • Keep your banking credentials secure
